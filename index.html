Where Innovation meets Art

This festival marks the convergence of five distinguished events — Prodigy, Epitome, Technotronix, Mebido, and Ignite — each of which has, over the years, cultivated its own unique identity and legacy. Prodigy represented the pursuit of excellence, Ignite embodied the spark of inspiration, Mebido showcased creative ingenuity, Epitome stood for the highest standards of achievement, and Technotronix celebrated technological advancement. With the conclusion of their journeys, these festivals have united to form a singular, comprehensive celebration. This new chapter is built upon their collective heritage, bringing together diverse disciplines, talents, and aspirations under one banner. It seeks to honor the traditions of the past while charting a forward-looking vision that fosters innovation, collaboration, and cultural enrichment.
